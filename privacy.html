Battuta — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 November 2026

Battuta collects no data.

What the app does not do

  • No analytics.
  • No ads.
  • No tracking.
  • No accounts or sign-up.
  • No third-party software that collects information.

Where your progress is kept

Your scores, streak and other progress are kept on your device. If iCloud is turned on, your progress also syncs through your own iCloud account so it follows you to your other devices. The developer cannot see or read it.

Apple services

Game Center leaderboards and the in-app purchase are provided by Apple and are covered by Apple's own privacy policy. Using Game Center is optional.

Reminders

The optional daily reminder is created on your device. Nothing is sent to a server.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages and collects no information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.
