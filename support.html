Battuta — Support

Battuta is a geography game for iPhone and iPad: flags, maps, capitals and routes between countries, with a daily challenge.

Contact

For help, questions or to report a mistake in the game, write to 87.limits_valid@icloud.com.

Common questions

How do I play? Open Settings in the app and choose How to play.

I bought Battuta Pro and it is not unlocked. Open Settings in the app and choose Restore purchase. Use the same Apple Account you bought it with.

My progress is missing on a new device. Progress syncs through iCloud. Check that you are signed in to iCloud on both devices and that iCloud is turned on for Battuta in iOS Settings.

How do I change the language? Open Settings in the app and choose Language.
