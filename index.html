How well do you really know the world?

Battuta is a geography quiz for iPhone and iPad with a new challenge every day. Guess flags, find countries on the map, name capitals, and work out which countries lie between two others.

Get Battuta on the App Store

Five ways to play

  • Flags — one flag, four countries. The wrong answers are look-alikes.
  • Map — name the highlighted country, or find a country on the map.
  • Capitals — match countries and their capitals.
  • Link — two countries are named. Which countries lie between them?
  • Mix — all four, shuffled.

A new challenge every day

Four rounds, the same for every player. Build your streak, earn streak freezes for the days you miss, and share your result as a picture that gives nothing away.

All 195 countries

Every flag, every capital and every border. Beat your best score and climb the Game Center leaderboards.

Private and offline

No ads, no tracking, no accounts. Battuta works without an internet connection and collects no data.
